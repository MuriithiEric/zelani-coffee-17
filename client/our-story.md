OUR STORY

IT STARTED WITH A CUP OF COFFEE.

Not a business plan.
Not an investment.
Not even a brand.

Just generosity.

After visiting Ethiopia — the birthplace of coffee — Patrick Githinji Muriithi returned to Kenya with new friendships, unforgettable experiences and an unexpected supply of exceptional coffee.

Friends he had made in Ethiopia would visit Kenya and bring him a kilogram or two of coffee. Before long, more than eight kilograms were sitting in his kitchen.

Patrick couldn't possibly drink it all.

So he gave it away.

Friends and acquaintances would ask if he had coffee, and he would generously give them a kilogram or two — free of charge.

Then they came back.

And asked for more.

They came back again.

And again.

By the third visit, Patrick recognised something.

This wasn't just generosity. It was demand.

People weren't returning because the coffee was free.

They were returning because they loved it.

That was when the entrepreneur in Patrick recognised an opportunity.

Zelani Coffee was born.

---

FROM ADDIS ABABA TO NAIROBI

In the beginning, Patrick sourced coffee directly from Addis Ababa and introduced it to customers across Nairobi.

The business was personal, relationship-driven and small.

Then came 2020.

COVID-19 disrupted international travel and supply chains, forcing Patrick to rethink the business.

Rather than stop, he adapted.

He began building relationships with Kenyan coffee entrepreneurs and exploring local sources.

When the world began returning to normal in 2021, he resumed sourcing from Ethiopia. But when Ethiopian coffee prices subsequently rose to unsustainable levels, Patrick faced a defining decision.

Continue depending on an increasingly expensive external supply — or build the future of Zelani around the extraordinary coffee available at home.

He chose Kenya.

---

ROOTED IN KENYA

Patrick began seriously sourcing Kenyan coffee and, in 2022, launched Zelani Coffee at the Organic Farmers Market in Karen, hosted at the Kenya Society for the Protection and Care for Animals.

It started with coffee bags.

Then came ready-to-drink beverages.

Then came loyal customers.

Then came a community.

What began with a few kilograms of coffee sitting in a kitchen had evolved into a growing Kenyan coffee brand.

Today, Zelani Coffee is one of the most sought-after coffee brands at the farmers market, now known as the Marula Green Market on Marula Lane in Karen.

But for us, this is only the beginning.

THREE PEOPLE. ONE VISION.

FOR THE FARMER

Exceptional coffee begins at origin.

We believe the farmer should not simply be the beginning of the supply chain, but an important part of the value created from it.

Zelani is committed to building a brand that celebrates the people, land and craftsmanship behind African coffee while creating stronger opportunities throughout the value chain.

Because great coffee deserves to create great value at origin.

---

FOR THE CUSTOMER

We want to change the way people experience coffee.

Our mission is to introduce more people to premium specialty coffee — coffee with character, provenance and a story worth knowing.

We want every Zelani experience to make someone pause and think:

“I didn't know coffee could taste like this.”

Because coffee shouldn't simply wake you up.

It should inspire you.

---

FOR THE INVESTOR

There is a much bigger opportunity behind the cup.

Africa produces some of the world's finest coffee, yet relatively few African coffee brands have achieved genuine global recognition.

We believe that gap represents an extraordinary opportunity.

Zelani is being built as a scalable African consumer brand with ambitions across specialty coffee, ready-to-drink beverages, cafés, hospitality, retail, distribution and international markets.

We are building the infrastructure, relationships and brand equity required to take Kenyan coffee from origin to global consumer.

For investors, the opportunity is not simply to invest in coffee.

It is to participate in the creation of a globally recognised African brand.
