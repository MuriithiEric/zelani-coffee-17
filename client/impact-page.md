ZELANI IMPACT 2030

From Farm. To Cup. To Future.

Our impact journey will grow alongside Zelani Coffee.

We are starting with approximately 50 farmers in 2026 and will progressively expand our farmer network as our sourcing, washing-station and market capacity grows.

Rather than pursuing growth at any cost, our objective is to build a strong, sustainable and measurable network of coffee farmers and communities.

Our 2030 SMART Objective

By December 2030, Zelani Coffee aims to work with approximately 400 farmers while creating measurable impact through better farmer value, financial literacy, environmental conservation, youth development, community support and responsible coffee tourism.

OUR 2030 TARGETS

1. FARMERS — 400+

Build a trusted network of approximately 400 farmers by 2030.

Our priority is not simply the number of farmers, but the strength and quality of the relationships we build with them.

2. BETTER FARMER VALUE

Once our washing stations are established, introduce a transparent quality-based farmer premium designed to return more value to farmers.

We will measure and report the premiums paid rather than making unsupported claims about farmer income.

3. FINANCIAL LITERACY — 400+ FARMERS

Provide financial literacy opportunities to farmers within our network, covering practical areas such as saving, budgeting, investment and management of seasonal income.

4. ENVIRONMENT — 4,000+ TREES

Plant or support the planting of at least 4,000 trees by 2030, with our tree-planting activities growing alongside our farmer network.

Where our capacity allows, we will aim to exceed this target.

5. CIRCULAR PACKAGING

Establish and grow a Zelani packaging-return program that rewards customers through loyalty points, discounts or other appropriate incentives.

We will measure the number of packages returned and responsibly recovered.

6. YOUTH — ZELANI BARISTA ACADEMY

Establish the Zelani Barista Academy and progressively train young people in coffee, barista skills, hospitality and entrepreneurship.

Our initial target is to train at least 100 young people by 2030, while reporting higher numbers if we exceed this goal.

7. COMMUNITY — GIVING BACK

Continue supporting the communities connected to our coffee-growing areas through practical initiatives, including our existing clothing and shoe donations to farmers and families.

By 2030, we aim to reach at least 500 families through community initiatives, while reporting the actual number reached if we exceed this target.

8. JOURNEY WITH US

Develop Journey With Us into a responsible coffee tourism experience connecting customers with the farms, people, culture and communities behind their coffee.

Our initial goal is to connect at least 250 people with coffee-growing communities by 2030.

OUR ACCOUNTABILITY

We believe impact should be measured, not simply claimed.

Each year, we will review our progress against these targets and report our actual results.

If we exceed a target, we will report the higher achievement.

Our targets are therefore not ceilings.

They are commitments we intend to beat.

We will measure:

Farmers reached
Farmer premiums paid
Financial literacy participants
Trees planted
Packages recovered
Young people trained
Families supported
People experiencing Journey With Us

OUR PROMISE

We are not promising to change everything overnight.

We are promising to start, measure, improve and grow.

Because meaningful impact is not created by making the biggest promise.

It is created by keeping the promises you make — and doing more whenever you can.
